# Notices

## National Information Assurance Standard and National Data Classification Policy

The National Information Assurance Standard v2.1 [IAS-NAT-INFA] and the National Data Classification Policy v3.0 are published by, and belong to, the National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA) of the State of Qatar. This project acknowledges NCSA as their source and owner.

The repository does not reproduce NCSA's wording. It uses control identifiers, baseline marks, section structure and page numbers so that each control can be found in the official text. Every summary, crosswalk entry and note is this project's own work. The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCSA, and the official documents remain the reference for compliance decisions.

## Law No. 13 of 2016 on Protecting Personal Data Privacy

The PDPPL obligations are summaries written by this project from the English text NCSA publishes. The Arabic text in the Official Gazette is authoritative. The 72 hour notification window comes from NCSA's guideline PDPPL-02050217E.

## NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

The subcategory outcomes are taken from NIST CSWP 29, a work of the United States government. Dash punctuation in one outcome (GV.OC-03) was normalized to commas. The Arabic renderings are this project's own.

## ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Clauses and Annex A controls are referenced by number and title only, for identification. The standard itself is available from ISO.
